Frequently asked questions

I can't redeem a deal

Check that the deal is still active (it has a time window and a capacity), that you are logged in and that you haven't redeemed it already: each deal can be redeemed once per person.

The QR code has expired

Each code is valid for 5 minutes. Tap "Generate another code" on the same screen.

A business didn't honour its deal

Report it from its profile (flag icon) with the reason "The deal isn't as advertised". We review it and, if it happens again, the business is suspended.

I don't get notifications

Check Profile → Notification settings and the system notification permission. "Nearby" only alerts you about flash deals within your chosen radius, at most 3 times a day.

I run a business and want to sign up

In the app: Profile → "Got a business? Register it". We review it within 24–48 h. You can also email us.
