Sources and licences

Almost everything on Klendar is published by businesses and organisations. In some cities we also show the council's listings, which councils publish as open data; we use match times from an external service and the weather forecast from a public service, and the list of public holidays comes from the official gazettes. Here's where each thing comes from and under which licence.

Each city's public listings

Every day we import the cultural and leisure listings from these sources and publish them on the “Public listings” account of each city (or on the council's own, if it's on Klendar). Klendar doesn't organise those events: we adapt the dates to the app's format, choose a category, shorten long texts and, as the sources don't come with photos under a clear licence, we use a generic image for the category. Each event's page shows its source, licence, update date and a link to the original. A council publishing open data doesn't mean it takes part in or sponsors Klendar.

If an event is wrong, has changed or has been cancelled, the source rules: Klendar updates it the next day. To ask us to remove something or a whole source, write to info@klendar.app.

Matches and televised events

Matches and events in every sport (football, basketball, Formula 1, MotoGP, handball, UFC…) and their times come from TheSportsDB (thesportsdb.com), under its paid plan, which allows them to be used crediting them as the source: “Sports data: TheSportsDB”. What doesn't come in on its own (tennis finals, Eurovision…) is added by Klendar by hand, and each bar can add what it's showing. We don't use their images or show the TV channel: what matters is whether the bar is showing it. Times can change: check with the bar.

Weather

“Based on the weather” uses the forecast from MET Norway (Norwegian Meteorological Institute), under a CC BY 4.0 licence.

Public holidays

We use the list of public holidays to ask businesses whether they open. We import it every Monday from October to January from these official sources, and someone at Klendar reviews it before it's used; for each holiday we keep the source it comes from. We adapt the data to the app's format (day, name and town). None of these bodies takes part in or sponsors Klendar.

Maps

Towns
