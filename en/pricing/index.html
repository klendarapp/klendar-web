The small print, in two lines

  • Trial. When you register your business you start with every feature for a while; after that it moves to Free unless you take a paid plan.
  • How you pay. Until we have card payments, plans are paid by bank transfer: write to info@klendar.app and we activate it.
  • Changing or leaving. Whenever you want, at the same address. No lock-in, no penalty.
  • What a «featured» slot is. A publication that shows at the top for a while. When a publication is featured, the app says so: it is not dressed up as a recommendation.
  • Your plan does not change what people see beyond that: the order is chosen by each person in the filters.

And for people using the app?

Free, and no account needed to look. You only sign up to redeem, save plans or get alerts.
