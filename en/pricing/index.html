Several venues?

The price is per venue, because each one has its own audience, its own codes and its own numbers. The first venue pays the full price and, from the second one on, each extra venue pays less:

Your first venue€19.90 a month
2nd to 5th€15 a month each
6th onwards€12 a month each, with a dedicated contact

All + VAT. For example, with 3 venues you pay 19.90 + 15 + 15 = €49.90 + VAT a month; with 6, 19.90 + 4 × 15 + 12 = €91.90 + VAT.

Publishing to several venues at once is included. You write the offer once, tick the venues you want and each one gets its own, with its own address and its own code; you see the numbers per venue and added up. We don't charge extra for saving you from typing it three times.

The small print

  • Trial. When you register your business you get 30 days with everything, no card and no automatic renewal.
  • How you pay. Until we have card payments, by bank transfer: email info@klendar.app and we'll activate it. In the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla, no VAT.
  • If you don't pay, your business is blocked until you do: nothing is deleted and you can still validate what people already have. We remind you 7 days and 1 day before.
  • Seasonal pause. Up to 6 months without paying; what you'd already paid for is kept for when you come back. Meanwhile, your business is stopped.
  • Cancel whenever you like: you keep everything until the end of the period you've paid for and it doesn't renew. No lock-in, no penalty; from the dashboard (“Leave Klendar”) or at the same address.
  • We never charge per redemption. No percentage, no euros per code: if it works well for you, you pay the same.
  • What “featured” means. A publication that shows at the top for a while; it's charged separately and you request it by email. When one is featured, the app says so: it isn't dressed up as a recommendation.
  • Your plan doesn't change what people see beyond that: the order is chosen by each person in the filters.

And for people using the app?

Free, and no account needed to browse. You only sign up to redeem, save plans or get alerts.
